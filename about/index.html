Ontology engineering and knowledge graphs

I define scope with established methods (NeOn, METHONTOLOGY, Ontology 101) and competency questions. I model with OWL 2 and property graphs, and I query with Cypher and SQL/PGQ. I have worked with Neo4j and Graph Data Science, TigerGraph, ArangoDB and Apache Jena Fuseki, and with GraphRAG libraries, LLM-based entity and relation extraction, and entity resolution.

I use the ontology as a governance layer: it defines which data, queries and actions are allowed. In OCOR, my current project, the ontology is kept as code and compiled into a versioned canonical model, and AI agents work only through typed, policy-filtered queries and tools.

Selected evidence

Implementation in development

OCOR

A sovereign, vendor-neutral semantic core that connects ontology definitions, evidence, governed decisions and controlled actions. I designed its ontology-as-code metamodel and built its ontology compiler: it type-checks YAML or JSON ontology sources, rejects unresolved or cyclic references, and turns each release into a deterministic, content-addressed canonical model with a semantic diff and a compatibility class. A breaking release is rejected without a migration plan. AI agents use only typed, policy-filtered named queries and typed tools, with budgets and a kill switch.

Limitation: an implementation in development on a wholly synthetic scenario, not a production system. Qualification is in progress.

Psychology-informed multi-agent systems

My academic training in Psychological Sciences gives me an engineering perspective on authority bias, conformity, groupthink, social loafing and overconfidence. I do not treat language models as humans, nor do I assume they have human intentions. Instead, I translate these dynamics into concrete failure risks, structural safeguards and testing strategies.

The designs I work on separate procedural control from reasoning authority, embed structured dissent and falsifiable objections, use blind reviews and external checks, test agent ablations, and compare multi-agent setups against single-agent baselines with equal compute. I measure accuracy, resistance to misleading prompts, error propagation, traceability and cost.

Scope: these are conceptual and experimental designs. They are not a validated framework or completed benchmark.

Research-led engineering method

Method

Core Question → Literature Review → Stated Assumptions → Success & Failure Criteria → Instrumentation → Validation.

I separate evidence, inference and hypothesis, and I set evaluation criteria before I claim results. I instrument systems to monitor quality, cost, latency and failure rates, and I revise architectural choices against observed results and known limitations.
